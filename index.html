Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to help you get playing.

Are Nexora Games free to play?

Yes. You can play the games available on Nexora Games for free in your web browser.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No download or installation is required. Open the website, choose a game and press Play.

Can I play on my mobile phone?

Yes. The website is designed to work on mobile phones as well as computers. Open it in a modern browser and use the on-screen controls where provided.

How do I learn the rules of a game?

Open a game card and expand the “How to Play” section to read its instructions, goal and a helpful tip before playing.

Where are my high scores saved?

Your personal records are saved in your browser's local storage on the device you used. They are not a shared online leaderboard, and they may not appear on another device or browser. Clearing browser data can erase them.

Why is the sound off when I first visit?

Sound may start muted until you enable it. Use the Sound button in the top bar to turn on music and game sound effects. Your browser may also ask you to allow audio playback.

How can I contact Nexora Games?

For questions or feedback, visit our Contact page and send us an email.
