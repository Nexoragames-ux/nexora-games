Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexora Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you visit https://nexoragames-ux.github.io/nexora-games/ and play our browser games. By using the website, you acknowledge this policy.

1. Information we collect

Nexora Games does not currently require visitors to create an account or provide a name, phone number, or payment details to play. Some information may nevertheless be processed automatically by the website or service providers, such as browser and device details, approximate location derived from network information, pages viewed, and interaction or usage data.

Game records, sound preferences, and similar settings may be stored locally in your browser using local storage. These values are generally kept on your device and are not a global player profile.

2. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the website, such as which pages are visited and general usage patterns. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and collect information such as online identifiers, device/browser information, and interaction data. We use these reports to improve the website and its games.

Google processes data according to its own terms and privacy practices. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google Analytics safeguards data.

3. Cookies and local storage

Cookies or similar technologies may be used by Google Analytics and other services that support the website. Local storage may remember game scores and preferences on your device. You can manage or clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Blocking these technologies may affect some website features or measurement.

4. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve Nexora Games.
  • To understand website traffic and game usage in aggregate.
  • To remember locally stored game records and preferences.
  • To respond when you contact us.
  • To help protect the website and maintain its security.

5. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate or measure the website, including Google Analytics. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies. The website may also link to third-party websites; we are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.

6. Data retention and your choices

Analytics data is retained according to the settings available in our Google Analytics property and Google's applicable policies. Locally stored game records and preferences remain in your browser until you clear them or they are otherwise removed. You can limit cookies through browser settings and use available browser privacy controls.

7. Children's privacy

Nexora Games is a general-audience gaming website and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the concern.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or privacy at Nexora Games, contact us at nexoragames7@gmail.com.
